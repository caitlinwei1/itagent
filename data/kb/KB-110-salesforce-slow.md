id: KB-110
title: "Salesforce is slow or pages time out"
systems: [Salesforce]
symptoms: [slow]
audience: employee
status: active
updated: 2026-07-22
---
1. Check the IT status page for an open Salesforce incident. If one covers the employee's location, link the employee to it instead of troubleshooting further.
2. If several people in one office are affected, it is usually the office network or proxy, not the employee's laptop.
3. Single user only: clear the browser cache for *.salesforce.com, disable browser extensions, try another browser.
4. Still slow for one user: escalate to Business Applications with the page names and timestamps.
