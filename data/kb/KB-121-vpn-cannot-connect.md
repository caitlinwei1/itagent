title: "GlobalProtect VPN will not connect"
systems: [GlobalProtect VPN]
symptoms: [error_message, login_failed]
audience: employee
status: active
updated: 2026-05-30
---
1. Make sure you can reach a public website (if not, the problem is the local network).
2. Check the IT status page for a VPN incident.
3. "Authentication failed": your Okta account may be locked (KB-101).
4. "Gateway unreachable": choose another gateway (US-Central, US-Direct, EU-West).
5. Still failing: escalate to Network with the error text and time.
