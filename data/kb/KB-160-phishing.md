audience: employee
status: active
updated: 2026-08-28
---
Did not click: use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook and delete the email.

Clicked a link or entered your password: this is a security incident.
1. Disconnect the laptop from the network (turn off Wi-Fi) if you downloaded or opened a file.
2. Do not try to clean it up yourself, and don't shut down or wipe the laptop: Security may need to inspect it. Until Security Operations contacts you, avoid signing in to other sensitive systems from it.
3. Security Operations must be contacted immediately (the helpdesk agent escalates as P1). They will reset credentials, revoke sessions and decide whether the device needs checking.
