Employees can reset their own password at https://acme.okta.example/reset with Okta Verify. If Okta Verify is not available, the helpdesk agent can send a reset link to the registered recovery email after verifying identity.

Password rules: 14+ characters, not one of the last 10 passwords.

After resetting, update the saved password on every other device (phone mail apps, second laptop), or those devices will lock the account (see KB-101).
