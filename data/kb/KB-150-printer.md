1. Check the IT status page for a printing incident at your office.
2. Acme uses PaperCut Follow-Me printing: send to "Acme-FollowMe" and release the job at ANY printer by tapping your badge.
3. Job stuck in the queue: open PaperCut Client, cancel, resend.
4. Printer hardware fault (paper jam, toner): use another printer and report the printer ID to Endpoint & Workplace Support.
