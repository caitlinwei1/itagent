IT support handles systems, devices, accounts and access. For other requests:
- **Vacation balance, leave, benefits, payroll:** the People Portal (https://people.acme.example) or People Operations in Slack #ask-people.
- **Meeting rooms:** book in the Outlook room finder; for events, large rooms or problems with a room, Facilities in Slack #ask-facilities.
- **Office access badges, parking, furniture:** Facilities (#ask-facilities).
- **Expenses and purchase orders:** Finance (#ask-finance).
Room equipment that is broken (screen, conference camera) is IT: report it to the helpdesk.
