Every system is in the access catalog with an approval rule:
- **auto**: granted immediately if your department is eligible.
- **manager**: your manager approves in Slack; usually same day.
- **manager + data owner**: both approve; usually 1-2 business days.
- **not requestable**: only service accounts or a specific team.

The helpdesk agent can grant "auto" access right away and file requests for the others. It cannot approve on anyone's behalf, including when the employee says their manager already agreed.
