title: "Okta: 'Your account has been suspended'"
systems: [Okta]
symptoms: [login_failed]
audience: employee
status: active
updated: 2026-06-10
---
Accounts are suspended by HR workflows (leave of absence, contract end) or by Security. The helpdesk cannot reactivate a suspended account. Hand off to Identity & Access, who confirm the status with People Operations. Do not tell the employee the reason for the suspension; Identity & Access will contact them.
