id: KB-154
title: "Zoom: no audio or camera"
systems: [Zoom]
symptoms: [error_message, other]
audience: employee
status: active
updated: 2026-02-20
---
1. In Zoom > Settings > Audio/Video, choose the right microphone, speaker and camera.
2. macOS: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone/Camera > allow Zoom.
3. Update Zoom to the approved version (6.5) from Self Service.
4. Need to join right now: join from the Zoom phone app or dial in with the number in the invite.
