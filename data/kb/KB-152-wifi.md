title: "Corporate Wi-Fi: cannot connect"
systems: [Corporate Wi-Fi]
symptoms: [disconnects, login_failed]
audience: employee
status: active
updated: 2026-04-11
---
Managed laptops join "ACME-CORP" automatically with a device certificate. Personal devices use "ACME-GUEST".
1. Forget ACME-CORP and restart the laptop to renew the certificate.
2. If the laptop clock is wrong, the certificate fails; set time automatically.
3. Still failing: escalate to Network with the laptop asset ID and office floor.
