title: "Outlook not sending or receiving, mailbox full"
systems: [Microsoft 365]
symptoms: [error_message, other]
audience: employee
status: active
updated: 2026-10-07
---
Each mailbox has three limits, shown in its mailbox record: past the warning limit Outlook shows a yellow banner; past the send limit it stops sending; at the full limit it stops sending and receiving.
1. Empty Deleted Items and Junk.
2. Use "Mailbox cleanup" > find items larger than 10 MB.
3. Turn on Online Archive: right-click the mailbox > Archive settings (moves mail older than 2 years).
Mail still not syncing with space free: escalate to Workplace Technology.
