The standard mailbox quota is 50 GB (CHG-4399). At 49 GB Outlook warns; at 50 GB it stops sending.
1. Empty Deleted Items and Junk.
2. Use "Mailbox cleanup" > find items larger than 10 MB.
3. Turn on Online Archive: right-click the mailbox > Archive settings (moves mail older than 2 years).
Mail still not syncing with space free: escalate to Workplace Technology.
